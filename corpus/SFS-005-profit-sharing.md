# SFS-005 — Mudarabah and Musharakah: Profit Sharing and Loss Attribution

## §1 Scope

§1.1 This document addresses the two principal partnership structures: *Mudarabah*,
where one party supplies capital and the other supplies management, and
*Musharakah*, where all partners contribute capital.

## §2 Profit and loss

§2.1 Profit must be shared according to a *ratio* of realised profit agreed in
advance. Stipulating a fixed monetary amount, or a percentage of the capital
contributed, converts the arrangement into a loan bearing interest. See
[SFS-001 §3.1].

§2.2 In a Mudarabah, financial loss is borne by the capital provider alone, unless
the loss results from the manager's negligence, misconduct, or breach of the agreed
terms. The manager's loss is their unrewarded effort.

§2.3 In a Musharakah, loss is borne strictly in proportion to capital contributed,
regardless of the profit-sharing ratio agreed.

§2.4 Profit may only be distributed once it is actually realised. Distributions made
before realisation are advances and must be reconciled.

§2.5 A guarantee by the manager of either the capital or a minimum profit is not
permissible, as it removes the risk that justifies the return.

## §3 Investment accounts

§3.1 An unrestricted investment account operated on a Mudarabah basis is
permissible, with the institution as manager and depositors as capital providers.

§3.2 Depositors must be exposed to actual investment losses. An account marketed as
Mudarabah in which the institution absorbs all losses is not a genuine Mudarabah.

§3.3 The institution's share must be expressed as a ratio of profit, not as a fixed
fee charged against depositors' capital.

## §4 Profit equalization

§4.1 A Profit Equalization Reserve or Investment Risk Reserve may be maintained to
smooth returns across periods, provided its existence, basis, and ownership are
disclosed to depositors and they consent.

§4.2 Smoothing may not be operated so as to produce, in substance, a guaranteed or
predetermined return. Where reserves are used to deliver a consistent target rate
irrespective of performance, the arrangement reproduces the defect at
[SFS-001 §3.2].

§4.3 The treatment of reserve balances on account closure must be specified in
advance.

## §5 Assessment criteria

§5.1 A profit-sharing product should be judged compliant where the sharing ratio is
agreed in advance, the return varies with realised performance, and the capital
provider genuinely bears loss.

§5.2 A product should be judged non-compliant where a minimum return or the capital
sum is guaranteed to the investor by the manager.

§5.3 A product should be referred for review where smoothing mechanisms are present,
since permissibility turns on whether disclosure is adequate and whether smoothing
operates as a de facto guarantee.

§5.4 A product should be referred for review where the institution's remuneration
mixes a profit share with fixed fees, since the fixed component may in substance
guarantee the institution a return irrespective of performance.
